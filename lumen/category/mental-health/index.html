An old Parkinson's drug eased the inability to feel pleasure in depression by 4 points on a standard scale, versus placebo

In a placebo-controlled trial of 82 adults with anhedonic depression, the dopamine drug pramipexole cut pleasure-scale scores 4.04 points more than placebo over 9 weeks.

A new brain stimulation therapy matched ECT for severe depression with less memory loss

About 48% of patients with severe depression responded to magnetic seizure therapy non-inferior to ECT in the first large head-to-head trial, but with substantially less memory impairment.

Peer-reviewed · The Lancet Psychiatry · n=236 · 16 June 2026
